cover23 remaining exact-four-cycle geometries. Counting negative quintics that
meet the common positive core in either missing diagonal gives counts(1,1)

upper bound from checkpoint107 within this actual catalog scope.

In every two-negative block, each outside triple contains exactly one apex;
the apices are different; and their ordinary parts are disjoint two-sets.
This condition is invariant under core-frame changes and permutations of the
eight ordinary outside coordinates. The independent reader obtains it directly
from every relevant catalog entry, including both missing diagonals. The other
negatives can impose additional restrictions; this is only a necessary filter.
