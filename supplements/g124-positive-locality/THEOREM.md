# Graph lemma and minimum-six-repair corollary

Alexander Richard Towell, with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance. Local preparation21 October2026; no public-disclosure date is claimed.

A3-uniform hypergraph is **free** if every four-set contains at most two edges. For a free graph G and a missing triple t, define B_G(t) to be the four-sets containing t and exactly two edges of G. These are precisely the nonfree quartets created by adding t. Say t is *positively localizable in Q* if B_G(t) is nonempty and every member is contained in Q. Nonemptiness also implies t is contained in Q.

**Lemma (relative to the published classification).** For every free124-edge3-graph G on14 vertices and every five-set Q, at most one missing triple is positively localizable in Q.

The classification input is Klas Markström and John Talbot, *On the density of2-colourable3-graphs in which any four points span at most two edges*, Journal of Combinatorial Designs18(2),105–114(2010), [DOI10.1002/jcd.20223](https://doi.org/10.1002/jcd.20223). Figure1 on page4 of the author's3 October2008 manuscript gives unrestricted extremal size126 and exactly75 isomorphism classes at size124. Its following paragraph defines the opt−2 column. This is an inherited theorem about unrestricted graphs; the manuscript's separate2-colourable table is not used. See [REFERENCES.bib](REFERENCES.bib) and [data/external-classification.json](data/external-classification.json). The cached primary PDF hash is recorded there; it is not bundled and its classification search is not rerun.

The packet contains75 literal graphs. Each verifier checks all124 edges are distinct valid triples and every quartet is free. Each computes an isomorphism invariant: for every unordered pair{i,j}, record the sorted endpoint degrees, pair codegree, and sorted profiles `(degree(k),min(codegree(i,k),codegree(j,k)),max(...),edge(i,j,k))` over remaining k; sort these pair records. Relabeling preserves the nested tuple. All75 full tuples are distinct, so the75 graphs lie in different isomorphism classes. Together with the published count75, they cover every class. A complete canonical labeling algorithm is unnecessary: distinct invariant values suffice for distinction; witness counts alone would not.

For each missing triple, the first verifier lists all obstruction quartets and then all five-sets containing their union. It explicitly excludes empty obstruction lists. The second verifier instead visits each saturated quartet once, distributes its missing faces, and scans every five-set directly. Both compare every native record against the supplied certificate, and both find maximum positive locality1. The property is invariant under vertex relabeling, so it holds in every class and proves the lemma relative to the external count.

**Minimum-six-repair corollary.** Let H be a130-edge3-graph on14 vertices with a cardinality-minimum deletion repair D of size6, and put G=H−D. For every five-set Q, at most one t in D has every obstruction of G+t contained in Q.

Indeed G is free and has124 edges. For each t in D, G+t must be nonfree: otherwise D minus{t} would be a five-edge repair of H. Thus its obstruction list is nonempty and the lemma applies. No statement that every130-edge graph has minimum repair6 is made.

In particular, suppose D_0 and D_Q are disjoint sets of H edges of sizes4 and2, respectively; D_Q is contained in Q; H−D_0 has no nonfree quartet outside Q; and D_0 union D_Q is a minimum six-edge repair. For either t in D_Q, `G+t⊆H−D_0`, so all obstructions lie in Q. Minimality makes each obstruction list nonempty, contradicting the lemma for the two distinct restored triples. Disjointness must be justified before using that inclusion. Positivity cannot be inferred from an oversized or merely inclusion-redundant repair.

This argument uses no degree sequence, candidate-function symmetry, solver outcome, singleton catalog, local truth table or Fourier reduction. Those enter only the separate dependent Boolean application. The lemma is an immediate consequence of an older, stronger project locality property: at most two localizable missing triples per five-set (including vacuous ones), and any such pair restores to a free126-edge graph. The current smaller certificate does not establish literature novelty. The classification belongs to Markström and Talbot; the implementation, explicit finite certificate, exposition and corollary are credited separately.
