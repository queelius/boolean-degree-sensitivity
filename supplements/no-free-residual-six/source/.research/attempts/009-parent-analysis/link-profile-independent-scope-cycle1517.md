## Premises and mathematical use

Accepted1424 binds the complete inverse-contraction sphere catalogs of orders
7 through12. Its179583 coverage transitions and underlying contractible-edge
induction are explicit inherited premises; this cycle does not replay them.
The actual native record at each original index is retained. New computation
reconstructs distinct edges and neighbor sets from the triangular facets,
checks two triangle cofaces per edge, every connected cyclic vertex link,
connectedness and Euler characteristic2, then counts literal graph neighbors.
The profile predicates use these neighbor degrees, independently of1516's
implementation. No1516 or old catalog constructor is imported.

For a genuine signed-star object, accepted1413/1453 gives13 vertices, S2 vertex
links, and exactly three exceptional edges forming one star. Positive edges
have tetrahedral valence3, negative edges6, all other edges4/5. The degree of a
vertex in the link of v equals the tetrahedral valence of the corresponding
edge of K. Therefore a nonroot positive tip has exactly one link vertex of
degree3 and every other link vertex degree4/5; a negative tip has exactly one
degree6 and the rest4/5; a nonroot vertex outside the three tips has only4/5.
These statements do not apply the ordinary predicate to the central root.

The graph degree of v is the order of its sphere link. Accepted minimum graph
degree6 and the13-vertex ambient set bound that order between6 and12. The scan
covers every order7..12. At order6, a negative-tip link is impossible because
a simple six-vertex graph has maximum degree5. A positive-tip six-vertex S2
is impossible by the independently accepted elementary1497 lemma. An ordinary
order6 link is retained: the octahedral sphere is an explicit literal control.
The finite scan plus these small boundary arguments therefore supplies
necessary allowed degree sets for the three nonroot vertex types.

This is a necessary geometric projection. It does not establish that the
old12512 relaxed rows already entail these restrictions. It neither asserts
realizability of any selected link inside a genuine signed-star object nor
imposes a coordinate symmetry on a function. No negative-tip, ordinary-vertex,
or positive-tip constraint is applied to any target model in this cycle.

## Controls and resource gate

Seven normal/optimized controls cover the ordinary octahedron, a stacked
octahedron with exactly one degree3 vertex, a literal one-degree6 sphere,
a hexagonal bipyramid with two degree6 vertices (rejected by the exact-one
predicate), noninvolutive coordinate relabeling, malformed/aliased masks,
missing or aliased native identities, and omitted/altered selected records.

The price is128 repetitions of a literal12-vertex20-face icosahedral control,
not a native catalog scan. The measured0.01796 CPU seconds gives a1.783-second

the frozen source proposal. Native execution is not repeated for coverage.

The written sphere/manifold reductions and historical completeness remain
inherited repository acceptance, not newly formalized or independently
rediscovered. No human-review, novelty or original Boolean-function claim is
