# Disjoint cubic-exception pairs in an ordinary derivative

Status: candidate necessary local model; no exclusion is claimed here.

Assume the same thirteen-variable derivative hypotheses as
`two-mixed-cubic-derivative.md`, with all ordinary quadratic coefficients
0/1, constant c in {1,2}, binary quartics, and exactly two disjoint
exceptional pairs. At least one numerator is -2; the other is -2 or +1.
Root at an endpoint of a -2 pair.

Every triangle has a baseline pair and hence incidence zero or two.
Each exceptional endpoint has exactly one incident exception. The
accepted one-six-edge argument makes each negative endpoint link a flag
sphere of order nine, ten or eleven, and excludes a split two-C3 edge
link. The one-three-edge argument in the mixed note makes each positive
endpoint link a sphere of order seven through ten. Ordinary links have
only degrees four and five. If an ordinary link split into two six-vertex
projective planes, its 42 forced edges would leave at most nine cross
edges. Every vertex needs a cross edge; negative endpoints need at least
three and positive endpoints at least one. If all eight ordinary vertices
had cross degree at least two, the cross-degree sum would be at least
24 (mixed) or 28 (two negatives), exceeding eighteen. A good cross-degree-
one vertex instead forces a universal cross neighbor and at least eleven
cross edges. Thus all links are spheres.

The root link has one degree-six vertex and all others degree four or
five. The complete source catalog gives exactly three possibilities:
order nine/index22, order ten/index185, order eleven/index762. The second
exception lies on two of the eleven coordinates different from the root
and its exceptional partner. All 55 placements are covered by exact
automorphisms of the fixed root link and permutations of root nonneighbors.
Every such permutation is explicitly checked on the complete face set;
the quotient does not rely on a graph-isomorphism hash.

The necessary models impose local flagness only at ordinary vertices
and negative endpoints. Positive endpoints are not assumed flag.
Finite contradictions require independent row reconstruction and the
coverage proof above before promotion. Special coefficient-two cases
are outside the scope of these models.
